Project Porch Light

Camera-free sensing to help families support older adults and people with disabilities, especially in private places like bathrooms, where a camera doesn't belong.

It's in testing now. Wi-Fi sensing can show movement in a room, but reliable fall detection and activity recognition are not proven yet. The long-term idea is learning a person's normal patterns and flagging prolonged inactivity or a possible fall. See the progress →
