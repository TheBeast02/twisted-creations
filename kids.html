Meet the Friends

Every friend brings their own special energy to the forest — and someday, each one may get a learning adventure of their very own.

Bramble the Bear

Big, friendly, and steady. Bramble walks here and there, meets everyone, and always ends the day in his comfy chair.

Brings: calm & kindness

Little Bee

The busy little guide of Book 2. She introduces every friend, brings them all together, and spells each new word out loud.

Brings: the words

Fox

Quick, curious, and a little bit silly. If there's a harmless mishap in the forest, Fox is probably right in the middle of it.

Brings: the giggles

The Twin Otters

Two splashy twins who swim, dive, and play in the stream — inspired by two very special grandchildren, Milo and Meah.

Brings: splashy play

Turtle

Slow, wise, and always right on time. Turtle's favorite words are “It's time for…” — time for a story, a snack, or the sun.

Brings: perfect timing

Little Bug

The smallest friend of all, tucked under a log. Little Bug proves you don't have to be big to belong in the story.

Brings: a big hello

How the Books Grow

Curious how a Bramble book gets made? Here's the journey so far — and a peek at what's sprouting next.

  1. Book 1

    Bramble finds his chair

    Bramble's Comfy Chair introduces Bramble, his porch, and his very first friend — and families start reading it aloud together.

  2. September 2026

    🌎 Bramble learns Spanish

    La silla cómoda de Bramble brings the same cozy story to Spanish-speaking readers, checked word by word with a native-speaker proofreader.

  3. November 21, 2026

    Little Bee leads Book 2

    Bramble Bear and the Busy Little Bee arrives on Amazon — nine new words, a whole forest of new friends, twin otters inspired by Milo and Meah, practice pages for tracing and writing, and a cozy fireplace ending by Bramble's chair.

  4. Any day now

    🎨 The Art Studio opens

    Book 2's game, Bramble's Art Studio, is in its final finishing touches — painting, slime colors, and movie replays of your own drawings.

  5. Growing next

    🌟 A friend, a book, a new idea

    The plan for the series: alternate learning books and pure-fun adventures, with each friend bringing their own energy — and maybe their own book. Numbers might be next… or the otters might splash off on an adventure first. Stay tuned!
