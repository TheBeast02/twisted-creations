Project Porch Light

Camera-free sensing to help families support older adults and people with disabilities, especially in private places like bathrooms, where a camera doesn't belong. The first use in mind is helping a family keep an eye on elderly parents, with privacy intact.

What's working in testing now

  • Wi-Fi sensing can show movement in a room.
  • Empty-room calibration has been done.
  • The sensing board is running and live Wi-Fi measurements are arriving on the laptop.

What's NOT proven yet — honestly

  • Reliable fall detection is not proven yet.
  • Reliable activity recognition is not proven yet.
  • Vital-sign accuracy and accurate body reconstruction are not demonstrated.

What's next

  • Learn activity patterns from labeled recordings.
  • Longer-term, build personalized baselines for a person, with change alerts for prolonged inactivity.
  • Keep testing in real rooms before making any safety claims.

Follow along

Updates will come on From the Porch as testing goes on.
